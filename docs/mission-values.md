# Gapwise: mission and values

Founder: Dara Whitfield. Written 2026-11-02.

## Mission

Help small dental practices keep their chairs full so the people at the front desk can finish on time and the dentist can keep the practice independent.

## Why me

I spent six years at a dental front desk. I know the sticky note, the phone tag and the feeling of watching a good morning go empty. I do not want to build something for chains. I want to build it for the practice where the office manager also orders the gloves.

## Who we serve

Office managers and owner-dentists at independent practices with 1 to 4 dentists, starting in the Portland area. Their patients on the waiting list are the second group we answer to: we text them only with their agreement.

## Values

1. **Patients are not a list to be mined.** We only text people who agreed to hear from the practice. If we are not sure a patient agreed, we do not text them. We never sell or share patient details, and we keep only what we need to send one text.
2. **Front-desk people are the customer, not the cost.** Gapwise should take the worst part of the day off their plate. If a practice tells us it makes their staff feel replaceable, we change how we sell it.
3. **Plain prices.** One price ($149 a month), month to month, no contract, no setup fee. A practice can stop whenever it wants and we say so on the first call.
4. **Say what we do not know.** If we cannot yet show that something works, we tell the customer. No made-up numbers on the page.
5. **Small and sustainable.** I would rather have 12 practices that stay than 100 that leave. I will not hire before the first 12 are paying.

## Lines I will not cross

- No texting patients who have not agreed.
- No promising a filled chair. We promise to try and to show every result.
- No pressuring a practice that says "not now".

## How I will know it is working

Office managers tell me they went home on time. Patients take the earlier slot and do not complain. Practices are still with us after three months.

## How these values show up in the work

- The consent check (value 1) is a gate before any offer text in the pilot.
- The price page and the first call say "month to month" (value 3).
- Results go to the practice as they are, including the empty ones (value 4).
- No hiring before 12 practices pay (value 5); the kill rule in the business case is the stop line: fewer than 4 paying by 2027-02-28.
