Who it is for

Office managers and owner-dentists at independent practices with 1 to 4 dentists, starting in the Portland area.

Gapwise was started by Dara Whitfield, who spent six years at a dental front desk. It is built for the practice where the office manager also orders the gloves, not for chains. The aim is that the front-desk team can finish on time and the dentist can keep the practice independent.

What Gapwise does not do

  • It does not promise a filled chair. We promise to try, and to show you every result.
  • It does not text patients who have not agreed to hear from your practice. If we are not sure a patient agreed, we do not text them.
  • It does not sell or share patient details, and it keeps only what is needed to send one text.
  • It does not pressure a practice that says "not now".
  • It does not come with made-up numbers. We have no results to show you yet, and we will say so if we cannot show that something works.

Questions about patients and texts

Which patients do you text?

Only people who agreed to hear from your practice. If we are not sure a patient agreed, we do not text them.

What if a patient does not want the texts?

A patient can reply STOP and the texts stop.

What do you do with patient details?

We never sell or share them, and we keep only what we need to send one text.

Is written consent needed in every state?

We do not know yet. It needs a lawyer or a practice that knows the rules. We will not guess, and we will not text a patient unless we are sure they agreed.

Can I stop?

Yes, whenever you want. It is month to month with no contract.
